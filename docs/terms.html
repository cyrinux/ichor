Terms of Service

Last updated 10 November 2026. Applies to the Ichor apps for Android and iOS, the free open-source builds and the Google Play version.

Ichor is free and open source. It has no server, no account and no ads. You use it on infrastructure you own or are authorized to administer, at your own risk: the app acts on your real clusters. On Google Play, optional purchases fund the features to build next.

What Ichor is

Ichor is a free, open-source mobile application that connects to Kubernetes, Talos and adjacent infrastructure you own or are authorized to administer. The app itself stores no account: what it can do depends entirely on the credentials and clusters you add on your phone.

What you agree to

By installing or using Ichor you agree to use it only against infrastructure you own or have explicit permission to manage, and to comply with the licenses of the components it talks to (Talos, Kubernetes, Argo CD, Flux, and so on). You are responsible for the actions you trigger with the app — scale, delete, reboot and apply operations act on your real clusters.

No warranty

Ichor is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind, to the maximum extent permitted by law. The author is not liable for data loss, downtime, misconfiguration or any damage arising from using the app or any operation performed through it. Always keep backups of what you manage.

No account, no selling, no ads

Ichor has no user accounts, no tracking and no advertising. It talks only to the endpoints you configure. See the privacy policy for the full list of what stays on your phone and what leaves it.

Google Play purchases

The Google Play version of the app offers optional purchases: a tip for the project as a whole, or backing one of the features open for funding. Payments are handled by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service; the developer receives Play's sales reports, not your name or payment details.

  • Backing a feature supports its development. It does not buy the feature or a delivery date, and no purchase unlocks or removes app functionality: Ichor is fully functional without paying anything.
  • Purchases are one-time and voluntary. They are non-refundable by the developer; refund requests follow Google Play's refund policy.
  • The app remembers on the phone which features you backed. Nothing about your purchases leaves the phone beyond what Google Play needs to process them (see the privacy policy).

Donations outside Play

The open-source builds show crypto addresses and a GitHub Sponsors link. These donations are equally voluntary, give no entitlement to features or support, and are non-refundable.
