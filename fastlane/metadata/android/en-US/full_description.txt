Your Talos cluster, from your phone.

Ichor is a free, open-source client for Talos Linux clusters. Check node health, read logs, watch etcd and get notified the moment a node goes down, without opening a laptop. It talks to the Talos API with the same Go client as talosctl, so there is nothing to install on the cluster and no server in between.

<b>See everything at a glance</b>
• Cluster overview: every node ready, not ready or unreachable, with unmet conditions, clock drift and available Talos upgrades
• Services and logs: service and kernel logs colored by level, repeats folded, live follow mode
• Events timeline: service changes, boot phases and address changes as they happen
• Live graphs: CPU, memory, network, disk and load every two seconds, plus the process list
• Pods, network interfaces, routes, DNS, open connections, disks and hardware
• etcd: members, leader, size and alarms, defragmentation and snapshots
• KubeSpan peers: state, endpoint, last handshake and traffic

<b>Alerts without a server</b>
The phone itself checks your cluster in the background and notifies you when a node or etcd is unhealthy. A home-screen widget shows the state of the cluster at a glance.

<b>Act when you need to</b>
• Restart a service, reboot or shut down a node, after typing its hostname to confirm
• Upgrade Talos one node at a time, refused when etcd would lose quorum
• Packet capture saved as a .pcap for Wireshark
• Debug shell on a node, cluster health check, machine config viewer
• Issue a read-only talosconfig for another device as a QR code, and export a kubeconfig

<b>Least privilege by design</b>
Ichor reads the roles of your talosconfig certificate and only shows what it is allowed to do. Give your phone an os:reader certificate and it stays strictly read-only. The config is encrypted with a key held in the phone's security chip (StrongBox or TEE), behind an optional fingerprint or PIN lock. Screenshot mode hides addresses and node names when you share your screen.

<b>Several clusters</b>
Import as many talosconfigs as you like, switch from the header, give each cluster its own name and color, and open any of them straight from the app icon.

<b>Private and open source</b>
Apache-2.0, no account, no ads, no analytics. Your talosconfig only talks to your nodes. An optional AI diagnosis can explain what is wrong and how to fix it: it is off by default, shows you the exact report first, and sends nothing until you ask.

Available in English, French, Spanish, Ukrainian, German and Italian.
